About Me

I'm an Eagle Scout turned EMT who moonlights as a magician. By day, I respond to emergencies and help people through some of their toughest moments. By night (and weekends), I create wonder and astonishment through sleight of hand and storytelling.

Whether I'm performing a card trick or responding to a 911 call, the goal is the same: to connect with people, make a difference, and leave things better than I found them.

When I'm not working or performing, you'll find me at Disney parks, exploring the intersection of magic, storytelling, and experience design. Because at the end of the day, it's all about creating moments that matter.
